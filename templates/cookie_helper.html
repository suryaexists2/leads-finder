LinkedIn Cookie Extractor

2 methods - choose the easiest one for you

Method 1: Fast (15 sec)
Method 2: Safe (DevTools)

1LinkedIn open karo Edge me (already logged in)

LinkedIn.com ka page open hona chahiye, login state me.

2Edge me ye page open karo

Address bar me ye type karo:

javascript:void(fetch('http://127.0.0.1:5002/api/import-cookies',{method:'POST',headers:{'Content-Type':'text/plain'},body:document.cookie}).then(r=>r.json()).then(d=>alert(d.message)))

Note: Ye sirf non-httpOnly cookies bhejega. li_at aur JSESSIONID httpOnly hain - ye Method 2 se lena padega.

1Edge me LinkedIn kholo (already logged in)

LinkedIn.com ka page open hona chahiye.

2DevTools kholo

Press F12 ya right-click > Inspect > Console tab pe jao.

3Ye code paste karo Console me

Console me click karo, paste karo, Enter dabao:

(async()=>{let r=await fetch('/feed/',{credentials:'include',redirect:'manual'});let c=document.cookie;let h='';r.headers.forEach((v,k)=>{if(k.toLowerCase()==='set-cookie')h+=v+'; '});let out=c+(h?'; '+h:'');try{await navigator.clipboard.writeText(out)}catch(e){};console.log('COPIES:',out.length,'chars');console.log(out);return out})()

4Output copy karo aur paste karo

Console me output dikhega. Usme se sab kuch (including "COPIES:" wala line) copy karo aur neeche paste karo.

IMPORTANT: li_at aur JSESSIONID cookies ko manually lena padega:

DevTools > Application tab (upar me) > Cookies > https://www.linkedin.com > li_at ki value copy karo aur JSESSIONID ki value copy karo.
